Architecture & Pipelines

Two-Pipeline Architecture & Manual Bridge

System Overview
Pipeline A: Public Website & Admin (/api/*)
  • Form
    Contact / About Form: POST /api/submit-lead.php → IndexedDB outbox (INQ- receipt) → MySQL leads.
  • Visit
    Visit Request: POST /api/submit-visit-request.php → Kabul Sat–Thu 07:00–20:00 → visit_requests.
  • Call
    Etisalat Click-to-Call: POST /api/initiate-call.php → bridges Call Center ↔ Customer → ctc_public_call_log.
  • Direct
    WhatsApp & AI Chat: wa.me/93782222279 (no DB) & /ai-chat/handoff.php → conversations.json.
Demo Boundary: Public leads do not auto-sync to staff-service. Call Center / Sales logs website inquiries into the Staff CRM via New Entry.
Pipeline B: Staff CRM & Android (/staff-api/v1/*)
  • Call Center
    /callcenter & callcenter-desktop: Intake, draft creation, phone dedup, Sales handoff.
  • Sales
    /sales & sales-android: Biometric login, 24h SLA follow-ups, site visits, deal closing.
  • Leader
    /sales-leader: Team funnel, agent conversion, SLA overtime monitoring.
  • Manager
    /management: Duplicate customer review & HR Contract issuance (sold).
  • Finance / CEO
    /finance & /ceo: Installment deposits, cross-branch inventory & assignments.

Data Integrity & Relations

Customer Deduplication, Branch & Property Relations

Staff Service ↔ Branch Manager
1 Phone Deduplication
  • Registry: crm_phone_registry normalizes every mobile to +937XXXXXXXX.
  • New Phone: Creates cc_customers (C000001) + crm_customer_owners.
  • Conflict: If owned by another agent, creates crm_customer_review_requests (pending_review) and alerts Manager.
2 Manager Resolution
  • Queue: Branch Manager opens /management duplicate review queue.
  • Approve: approved_shared writes crm_customer_shared_access so both agents can work.
  • Reject: rejected protects the original owner's relationship.
3 Branch & Property Lock
  • Branch Scope: Project must match the staff member's branch_id.
  • Agent Scope: Sales must be assigned in inv_project_sales_agents.
  • 1-Lead Rule: crm_customer_property_leads PK (customer_id, property_kind, property_id) blocks duplicate unit claims (HTTP 409).

Native Android Capabilities & Field Workflow
  • Security
    BiometricPrompt + Encrypted Vault: BiometricAuthBridge.kt unlocks credentials via Fingerprint/Face and silently re-authenticates POST /auth/login.
  • Real-Time
    Polling & Deep-Link Alerts: Polls every 60s (or 15s when offline items await sync), plays a 2-tone chime, and opens /sales/leads?id={leadId}.
  • Comms
    One-Tap Native Intents: MainActivity.kt intercepts tel: → Android Dialer and wa.me/ → native WhatsApp. (No SMS button.)
  • Offline
    Field Resilience: offline-cache.mjs caches snapshots and queues lead_entry & followup_feedback mutations until connectivity returns.
  • Camera
    File Chooser: Supports native camera capture & gallery upload via FileProvider.

Marketing Roles & Obligations (mkt_production_entries)
  • Submitter
    Submits property (POST /marketing/entries) with customer name, address, and type within branch scope.
  • Manager
    marketing_manager / production_manager schedules filming date & assigns videographer / photographer.
  • Videographer
    Completes on-site shoot and marks filming_completed.
  • Editor
    Sets editing deadline, defines contentType, uploads exactly 5 distinct photos, serial & platform (Facebook | YouTube | Instagram).
Bridge to Public Website (submissions/retry)
  • Outbox
    Marking an entry posted writes a payload into mkt_submission_outbox.
  • Public Sync
    POST /submissions/retry pushes the 5 photos & property details to the public website submissions queue (/admin/submissions) for publishing, which then drives inbound leads back to Stage 1!
