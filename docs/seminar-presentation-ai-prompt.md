## Master End-to-End Business Flowchart (Include on Slide 2)
Draw a clear left-to-right visual flowchart showing every department handover:

1. **Engineering & CEO** prepare project blueprints, 3D renders, and available apartment units.
2. **Marketing** films, edits, and publishes property listings (with 5 verified photos) across Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and the Website.
3. **Customer Inquiry** arrives via Website Form, Private Visit Booking, Click-to-Call, WhatsApp, AI Chat, or Branch Walk-in.
4. **Call Center** logs the customer, checks for duplicate phone numbers, qualifies their budget/needs, and assigns the lead to a **Sales Agent** (starting a **24-hour response timer**).
   - *If the customer already belongs to another agent:* The system pauses and sends a **Duplicate Review** to the **Branch Manager** to approve sharing or protect the original agent.
5. **Sales Agent (Android App & Web)** receives an instant mobile alert, calls or WhatsApps the customer with one tap, schedules a **Site Visit**, and logs every follow-up.

   - *If the customer agrees to buy:* **Only a Sales Agent** can mark **Deal Closed**, lock the apartment unit, and upload the **Buyer Photo** and **Tazkira (National ID)**.
6. **Branch Manager & HR** verifies the buyer's ID, project sellers, construction company, and legal boundaries, then issues the **Official Sales Contract** (marking the unit **Sold**).
7. **Finance Department** tracks the issued contract and records/approves **Installment Deposits** in USD or AFN.
8. **Sales Leader & CEO** monitor live conversion funnels, agent performance, and 24-hour response compliance.
