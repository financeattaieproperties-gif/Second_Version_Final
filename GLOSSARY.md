# Attaie Properties

Attaie Properties operates a public property website and separate internal staff workspaces.

## Language

**V1**:
The existing live Attaie Properties installation and its existing business records.

**V2**:
The replacement website and staff workspaces being prepared for a single complete release on `attaieproperties.com`.

**Website Admin**:
The administrator responsible for public listings, website inquiries, messages, and publication review.
_Avoid_: CEO, Staff Admin

**CEO**:
The internal staff role with global oversight across branches. This role is distinct from Website Admin.

**Production launch**:
The transition of `attaieproperties.com` to the verified V2 release, preserving existing live business data.
_Avoid_: fresh installation, testing deployment
